About this study

An original, texture-free procedural reconstruction guided by a supplied photograph: a dark cave opening frames a beach and two offshore sea stacks. Shapes, surface detail, clouds and surf are generated mathematically; no photograph or external texture is sampled.

The reference photograph is not redistributed here. Its location, authorship and license have not been verified, so this study makes no geographic attribution or claim of photographic fidelity.

This is a 2.5D composition with approximate depth and subtle pointer parallax, not free-camera geometry. Waves, foam and wet sand suggest a shoreline; there is no physical tide or erosion simulation.

Other light and weather settings are fictional artistic variations, not documented conditions. Sea state and weather are independent controls. This version has no rain or snow overlay.

The clock shows your local time. Scene time changes lighting only; the 60-second day preview does not speed up the surf or clouds. Space pauses, F toggles fullscreen, and H hides the interface. Opening this dialog suspends rendering.
